# North-Star Roadmap — from platform to park

Written 2026-07-23, as the durable reference the ladder is run against.
The park is the fixed end. Everything below is backwards from it.

> **The XR theme park is the destination. The venues are the means.**
> Heroes built the identity kernel, the LBE platform, and the partner
> venues *to reach and justify the park* — not as an end in themselves.
> Do not let the instrument become the goal.

---

## The conviction that governs every decision

**Wow the analog, elevate with the technology.**

Humans are all senses — touch, smell, taste, proprioception — not just
sight and sound. XR loses society's imagination the moment it loses its
grounding in the other senses. Meta's ~$80B error was leading with the
technology and asking humans to accept a sensory-impoverished world
because the tech impressed. Rest on tech alone and you become a novelty
that shuffles into obscurity within two years.

This is the same discipline that produced everything good so far: **never
lead with the expensive, unproven thing.** Wow the analog first (proven,
universal, grounded); elevate with technology second (expensive,
fragile).

### The economics that follow — render AND build

NOT "software margins replace concrete." That is the Meta trap wearing a
spreadsheet. The real thesis:

- The physical build is **both the moat and the wow.** The analog must be
  genuinely exceptional and stand on its own.
- The content layer is what lets one superbly-crafted acre become a dozen
  worlds over its life, and evolve weekly, **without re-pouring concrete.**
- Versus Disney: their physical is fixed the moment it is poured; a
  re-theme is a demolition. Heroes' physical is a stage that re-dresses
  in software. **Beats Disney on evolvability, beats Meta on grounding.**

This makes the park *truer, harder, and more capital-intensive* — never
cheaper. It adds disciplines Heroes does not yet have in the building:
experiential and scenographic design, sensory design, physical safety
and liability.

## The ladder — each rung buys the right to climb the next

### Rung 1 — De-risk AR cheaply (a *fusion* proof)
The smallest possible proof, using Heroes' own contract-first method
(the spatial skeleton — Slice 4 — already exists before any runtime).
**Not a bare AR proof: a fusion proof.** One room — a real physical set,
one tactile element, one environmental cue (scent / temperature /
texture), and the AR layer — combined, with guest response measured at
the same rigor as everything else (Slice 6 telemetry is the harness).
Prove the *ratio* of analog to technology cheaply, before funding it at
scale. Thousands, not millions. Weeks, not a raise.

### Rung 2 — Prove the engine
A **tight** cohort of software venues with a real control (partner's
historical baseline, or intra-venue A/B). Sized for undeniable
retention / margin signal — **not** a 300-venue SaaS empire. This rung is
instrumental evidence, not a company to fall in love with.

*Evidence artifact:* a one-page cohort readout — repeat-visit lift,
walk-in conversion (`claims ÷ guest seats`), per-guest margin, versus
baseline. Write this template **before** the pilot, so success is
measured on purpose.

### Rung 3 — The flagship
**One** Heroes-operated space, the full AR + analog fusion, the vision
made real and *ours*, at survivable acreage (a leased warehouse, dead
retail, a partner's wing — the tech and the crafted set carry the space;
no ground-up Disney build to start). This is the rung that was missing
from the first plan. It turns "a software company with a dream" into
"the company that has already built and operated a smaller version of the
dream." It proves Heroes can operate *physically* — the thing no
venue-SaaS metric proves — and it showcases the render-and-build
economics live.

### Rung 4 — The park
Raised off engine economics + flagship operating proof + the fusion
thesis. Structure the physical capital — developer JV, project finance,
or an operator partner who carries the balance sheet — so Tim keeps the
creative throne and a controlling stake. **He builds it; others carry the
concrete.** This is not licensing the vision away; it is the standard
structure by which every visionary megaproject gets financed while its
author keeps control.

## Encode the convictions as gates, not vibes

A conviction that stays philosophy gets value-engineered out the first
time a spreadsheet objects. Heroes already built the machine to hold the
line: the **certification gate** (Slice 9) and the **telemetry
thresholds** (Slice 6).

- **Spatial accuracy** already gates today (drift, localization, fps).
- **Multisensory craft** must join it: a "Heroes experience" certifies on
  physical set, tactile feedback, and environmental design **as well as**
  spatial accuracy — never spatial alone.
- **Ethical monetization** is the same shape: no loot boxes, no gambling,
  spending caps in the player's hands.

If it is a gate, "uncertified blocks the run" protects it. If it is only
in a founder's head, it is the first thing cut. This is the soul-steward
role made operational.

## Capital, honestly

$5.2M / 24 months was planned to reach the first POC. Heroes hit 90–95%
of it in under three months for under $3,000 — an inversion of the
category's defining failure mode (capital incineration; Magic Leap burned
$3.5B). That efficiency is a **method**, not luck: build the contract
first, validate cheaply, scale only what proves out.

The honest scope: that $3K de-risked **software** — Heroes' strongest
domain. It did *not* de-risk the AR experience, the guest-pays-and-returns
market question, or the 2D/3D asset pipeline — and those are exactly where
the next capital goes and where the industry dies. **The instruction is to
apply the identical de-risk-before-scale method to AR and content** — the
fusion proof of Rung 1 — rather than hand a war chest to a firm to build a
full experience on faith.

*(This document sets direction, not financial or investment advice. Specific
raise sizes, valuations, and deal structures are for Tim and qualified
advisors.)*

## Roles

- **Tim — CEO, vision and soul.** The operational core of that role: the
  person who kills the meeting when someone says "just add loot boxes,
  the numbers go up." The mission survives only as encoded, testable
  commitments that someone with the title and conviction defends.
- **COO / President — day-to-day.** The commercial and operating grind.

Failure mode to guard against: "steward the vision" quietly becoming
"avoid the unglamorous commercial work." The soul is protected *by*
holding specific lines — the gates above — not by holding the vision in
the abstract.

## The mission

The park is the anti-loot-box at maximum surface area: real-world social
play, persistent meaning, grounded in every human sense, with no dark
patterns. "Positive change in the world" and "fundable" are the same
thing here by design — for the right partner and the right capital, the
mission *is* the reason to say yes.
